Is this right for your family?

Who this is for — and who it's not

✅ This is for you if…

  • Your 9–16-year-old spends hours watching, scrolling and gaming, but has never built one thing of their own.
  • You want your child to use AI to create, safely, not just consume what others make.
  • You can give 30 minutes a day for seven days, and want something finished to show at the end.
  • You're not tech-savvy and want a step-by-step path your child can follow on their own.
  • You care about focus, confidence and the courage to try again, not just tech skills.

🚫 This is not for you if…

  • You're still deciding whether AI is even worth teaching a ten-year-old.
  • You want a ready-made AI project, not a child who learns to build.
  • Your days are packed and you can't spare 30 minutes daily for seven days.
  • Your 16-year-old already codes in Python and ships apps alone.
